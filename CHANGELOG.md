## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-17

### Added

- Xiaomi TVs running MIUI TV / PatchWall found on the local network, without any account or pairing: an SSDP search through the network discovery of Gladys, then the port 6095 API of each address, plus IP addresses typed in the configuration; each TV is identified by its MAC address, found again after an address change, and remembered in `/data`.
- One device per TV with On/Standby (assumed: the last command, on when the TV answers again, off when it stops answering), Volume from 0 to the maximum of the TV, and ten remote keys shown as push buttons (volume up, volume down, mute, arrows, OK, menu, back).
- Volume reached with the volume keys and read back after each burst; Mute lowers the volume to 0 and restores it.
- Wake-on-LAN through Gladys to switch on a TV that no longer answers, when its MAC address is known.
- States read every 10 s to 1 min, within the Gladys rate limit, with a local or unreachable badge per TV.
- Dashboard widget "Xiaomi TV": state, volume, the first six applications with their icons, and the buttons Standby / Switch on, Home, Mute and Open an app (a form listing every application).
- Scene actions: open an application by name or package name, press a sequence of keys, switch to an HDMI input.
- Configuration button "Search and test the TVs".
- Texts in English, French, German and Spanish.
